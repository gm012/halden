Who We Are

More than documentation.
We create building certainty.

Halden is a Building Documentation & Council Support Consultancy specialising in commercial building documentation and council support.

Buildings naturally evolve. Spaces are renovated. Tenants change. Extensions happen. Building use changes.

However, documentation does not always evolve with them.

Our role is to identify these gaps, rebuild accurate building records, and provide clients with the information required to make confident decisions.

Why We Exist

Buildings change.
